# 2026-10-16 progress overview

## The fake-rally filter (D4) checked on 36 new sessions: borderline

The filter throws out a detected rally when the "ball" in it almost never flies like a ball. Ran it,
settings frozen, on 36 PGC sessions it had never seen. Detail: `EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-10-16.

- **What it did:** dropped 12 of 329 rallies (4%), so it is gentle.
- **Were they junk?** Watched all 12 in CVAT. Final verdict: 9 junk, 3 real rallies (d-03, d-05,
  d-09). The bar allowed at most 3 real, so it passes, exactly at the limit.
- **Caveat:** the first pass said 5 real; 3 were changed to junk on a second look after seeing
  that the first pass failed. Recorded both.
- **Its score can't tell the 3 real ones apart from junk:** one real rally scored 0.0, so a
  different threshold would not help.
- **Unknown:** how much junk it *keeps*; only dropped clips were reviewed.
- **Not switched on.** Next, if pursued: review a sample of the rallies it keeps, to see whether
  removing ~4% of rallies makes a visible difference to reels at all.

## Bounce finding fails on a second camera: the tracker gets stuck on light reflections

Labelled every bounce in a 4-minute recording from a different court (36 bounces, CVAT project
`bounce-chunk2-pgc`). Detail: `EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-10-16 (bounce chunk 2).

- **Result:** the zoomed-in detector finds 4 of 36 (11%). Bar 40%. Fails; the first court's 42%
  does not carry over.
- **Why (a lead, from the numbers and one still frame):** the tracker locks onto fixed glints, mainly
  a light reflection on the court surface and a white object on the back wall. Over half of
  the "ball" points sit on one spot, so the real ball is ignored.
- **What it means:** the problem here is the background, not zoom. This is the same
  fixed-bright-spot problem that has beaten every fake-rally fix so far.
- **Not done:** the bounce-vs-hit speed check on this court (too few candidates to score).

## Later: the glare is a main cause of the missed bounces, but not the only one

- **Removing the 4 fixed glare spots before tracking:** bounces found go from 4 to 10 of 36 (28%).
  None of the labelled bounces sat on a glare spot, so nothing was lost doing it. Still under 40%.
- **Playback review of 14 missed bounces:** glare is in nearly every clip; at the moment of the
  bounce it is in the track in half of them. The other half miss for another reason, in the bounce
  rule itself.
- **What it means for the tracklet idea:** glare points really are corrupting the track, so judging
  short tracklets and dropping still ones before committing is worth building. Unlike a fixed pixel
  mask, it would not delete a real ball passing over a reflection. It would not fix the second half.
- **Next options:** (1) build tracklets-first and test it on both chunks and on rallies (rallies are
  where masks failed before); (2) look at why the bounce rule misses well-tracked bounces.

## Test E: the tracklet idea, first version, fails narrowly; the first version was too lenient

- Built "tracklets first": link detections into short tracklets, drop the ones that sit still,
  then track as before. Fixed settings, bar committed first.
- **Bounces:** second court 4 -> 6 of 36 (bar 10; the blunt mask got 10). First court unchanged (14).
- **Rallies:** removes 6 of 8 known glare fakes (best so far) but loses 4 of 60 real rallies (bar 3).
- **Why weak:** it removed only 41% of the glare. With glare on half the frames, glare points got
  chained into tracklets with real ball points, and anything "moving" was protected.
- **Next, if pursued:** judge stillness point by point (or cut tracklets at still spots), tested on a
  third camera with its own labelled stretch, since the second court is now used up for diagnosis.

## Test E2: the point-by-point version also fails, it throws away slow balls

- Labelled a third stretch (eda301d9, 26 Sept, 4 minutes; 98 bounces, CVAT project
  `bounce-chunk3c-pgc`). Detail: `EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-10-16 (Test E2).
- **Glare:** the new version removes 87% of the glare on the second court (the first removed 41%),
  and it removes all 8 known glare fake rallies.
- **But it does harm where there is no glare:** on the new stretch it loses 4 bounces the current
  system finds (34 -> 30; allowed to lose at most 1), and it loses 4 of 60 real rallies (limit 3).
- **Why:** a slow ball, a soft bounce near the kitchen or a ball dribbling to a stop, stays on
  nearly the same spot for a moment and looks like a glint to the rule.
- **Good news on the side:** on a court without glare the current bounce finder gets 35%, close
  to the 40% bar. The second court's poor result was mainly glare.
- **Not switched on.** Next, if pursued: a rule that only removes spots that stay lit for minutes
  (a glint) rather than seconds (a slow ball). That needs fresh labelled footage to test honestly.
