# 2026-10-16 progress overview

## The fake-rally filter (D4) checked on 36 new sessions: borderline

The filter throws out a detected rally when the "ball" in it almost never flies like a ball. Ran it,
settings frozen, on 36 PGC sessions it had never seen. Detail: `EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-10-16.

- **What it did:** dropped 12 of 329 rallies (4%), so it is gentle.
- **Were they junk?** Watched all 12 in CVAT. Final verdict: 9 junk, 3 real rallies (d-03, d-05,
  d-09). The bar allowed at most 3 real, so it passes, exactly at the limit.
- **Caveat:** the first pass said 5 real; 3 were changed to junk on a second look after seeing
  that the first pass failed. Recorded both.
- **Its score can't tell the 3 real ones apart from junk:** one real rally scored 0.0, so a
  different threshold would not help.
- **Unknown:** how much junk it *keeps*; only dropped clips were reviewed.
- **Not switched on.** Next, if pursued: review a sample of the rallies it keeps, to see whether
  removing ~4% of rallies makes a visible difference to reels at all.

## Bounce finding fails on a second camera: the tracker gets stuck on light reflections

Labelled every bounce in a 4-minute recording from a different court (36 bounces, CVAT project
`bounce-chunk2-pgc`). Detail: `EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-10-16 (bounce chunk 2).

- **Result:** the zoomed-in detector finds 4 of 36 (11%). Bar 40%. Fails; the first court's 42%
  does not carry over.
- **Why (a lead, from the numbers and one still frame):** the tracker locks onto fixed glints, mainly
  a light reflection on the court surface and a white object on the back wall. Over half of
  the "ball" points sit on one spot, so the real ball is ignored.
- **What it means:** the problem here is the background, not zoom. This is the same
  fixed-bright-spot problem that has beaten every fake-rally fix so far.
- **Not done:** the bounce-vs-hit speed check on this court (too few candidates to score).
