# 10.04 progress overview

## Booking systems can now drive the cameras (built, not yet deployed) — PIC-191, ADR-151

A prospective venue books courts on TinyBook, which copies every booking into a Google Calendar. Tested with a TinyBook trial: each calendar event reliably names the court (`場地 2`), has real start and end times, and also contains the customer's name.

**What was built** (on branches, three repos, not pushed):
- **Players can now claim reels from any session on their court**, not just ones they started by scanning. This fixes a real existing gap: a session the venue scheduled in the console recorded a reel nobody could claim. Booked and venue-scheduled sessions can be joined from their start until 30 minutes after they end, by anyone who scans the court QR (up to 10). Nobody "hosts" them, so nobody can end or extend them early.
- **Settings → Booking calendar** in the console: sign in with Google, pick the calendar, match each court to a camera, turn on. Bookings then start and stop the right camera automatically; edits and cancellations follow within about a minute.
- Only the court and times are kept, never customer names.

**Verified:** unit tests (console 253, reel 15), console production build, and both database changes run against production inside transactions that were rolled back — nothing was saved.

**Still needed before it's live:**
1. Create the Google OAuth client (Google Cloud), set `GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_ID` / `GOOGLE_OAUTH_CLIENT_SECRET` on Netlify, publish the app "In production", start Google's verification.
2. Push the branches, open PRs, apply the two migrations, deploy.
3. TinyBook trial round 2 (edit, move court, cancel) to confirm how cancellations arrive.
4. End-to-end test with the trial calendar, and check the QR page on a real phone.
