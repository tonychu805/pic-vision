# 10.04 progress overview

## Booking systems can now drive the cameras (built, not yet deployed) — PIC-191, ADR-151

A prospective venue books courts on TinyBook, which copies every booking into a Google Calendar. Tested with a TinyBook trial: each calendar event names the court in TinyBook's provider field (`服務提供者: Court 1` — the venue sets up one provider per court), has real start and end times, and also contains the customer's name.

**What was built** (on branches, three repos, not pushed):
- **Players can now claim reels from any session on their court**, not just ones they started by scanning. This fixes a real existing gap: a session the venue scheduled in the console recorded a reel nobody could claim. Booked and venue-scheduled sessions can be joined from their start until 30 minutes after they end, by anyone who scans the court QR (up to 10). Nobody "hosts" them, so nobody can end or extend them early.
- **Settings → Booking calendar** in the console: sign in with Google, pick the calendar, match each court to a camera, turn on. Bookings then start and stop the right camera automatically; edits and cancellations follow within about a minute.
- **Booking number and booker name are kept** (operator decision): players see the name masked (`A*****y`) with the booking number on the QR page so they can spot their own session; the venue sees the full name in the console (Schedule, Reels). Names are deleted after 90 days. **The privacy policy and venue notice need updating before this goes live.**

**Verified:** unit tests (console 253, reel 15), console production build, and both database changes run against production inside transactions that were rolled back — nothing was saved.

## Player account deletion + privacy policy retention (built, not deployed)

Checking what a player's account stores showed the privacy policy never said how long account data is kept, and there was no deletion procedure.
- **Console, branch `player-account-deletion`:** `delete_player_account` plus `pnpm delete:player <id|email> [--apply]`. Deletes the account, sessions joined, consents and the Supabase/Auth0 sign-in. Keeps the consent proof (version, time, IP, browser, no name) for 5 years. Fixed: players who had ever started a QR session couldn't be deleted (foreign key with no ON DELETE). Tested in a rolled-back transaction; not run on a real account.
- **Website, branch `privacy-account-retention`:** accounts kept until a deletion request (deleted within 30 days), consent proof kept 5 years, and the policy now discloses that venues see members' name, email and history.
- **Website, branch `pic-191-privacy-booker-names`** (on top of the above): booker-name disclosure. **Publish only with the booking-calendar release.**
- The 30-day and 5-year figures are my reading of PDPA arts. 13 and 30, sent to the lawyer on PIC-190 along with inactive-account deletion and the lawful basis for booker names.
