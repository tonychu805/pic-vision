# 10.19 progress overview

## Paddle hits: a finder built on the ball's path doesn't work on new play

- **What was tested:** a hit finder that flags any sharp turn or sudden speed-up in the tracked ball (not only
  the bounce-like dips the bounce finder looks for). The rules were written down using minute 1 of the
  operator's hit marks, then tested once on minute 2, which had been kept back.
- **Result:** on minute 1 it found 7 of 22 hits. On minute 2 it found **2 of 22**, and only 2 of its 27 boxes
  were hits. Failed the bar set in advance.
- **Why:** the ball's path is broken exactly where the paddle meets the ball. Near the camera the ball is
  almost never seen at contact (0 of 9 near hits usable in minute 2). On the far side the tracker jumps or
  loses it, and most boxes land on wobble in the track rather than on hits.
- **Decision (ADR-156):** stop tuning rules on the ball's path for hits. Hits come back only with a better
  ball track near the players, or with a cue that doesn't need the ball (where the wrist is, when the arm swings).
- **Kept:** all 44 hit marks (`eval/labels/hits/`), the test set for whichever of those comes next.

## Paddle hits: the arm swing works where the ball doesn't

- **What was tested:** instead of the ball, watch the players' wrists. A hit is a fast arm movement, and the
  pose model sees the arm even when the ball is blurred or hidden. Rules written down on minute 1, tested
  once on minute 2.
- **Result:** found **12 of 22 hits** on minute 2, including **4 of 9 near-side hits** (the ball-based finder
  got 0 of those). About 1 in 4 swings it flags is a real hit; random timing would find about 3 hits.
  Passed every check set in advance, but only just.
- **What it means:** it's the first workable route to counting hits, especially near the camera. It isn't
  a hit counter yet: most swings it flags are players moving without hitting.
- **Next:** combine the swing with the ball (a swing when the ball is close to that player), and check on a
  third minute or another court before relying on it.

## Paddle hits: arm swing + ball check passes on a fresh minute

- **What was tested:** the arm-swing finder, keeping a swing only when the ball is in play and isn't clearly
  somewhere else. Rules fixed before the operator marked minute 3, then tested once on it.
- **Result on minute 3 (27 hits):** found **18** (near side **7 of 12**), and **more than half** of what it
  flagged were real hits (18 of 32). Random timing would find about 2. Passed all five checks set in advance.
  The plain swing finder also held up a second time (19 of 27).
- **What it means:** we now have a working hit finder on this court: about 2 in 3 hits found, about 1 in 2
  flags right. Good enough to start building hit counts and shot timelines, with visible error; not a referee.
- **Decision (ADR-157):** hit work builds on this finder from now on.
- **Next:** check it on another court (the glare court) and with the production detections before relying on it.
