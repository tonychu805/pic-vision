# 10.17 progress overview

## Glare filter: what we learned and what we decided (ADR-154)

Wraps up yesterday's bounce and glare work. Detail: `DECISIONS.md` ADR-154 and `EXPERIMENTS.md`
2026-10-16 (Test E2 and its follow-up).

- **Learned:** fixed shiny spots (a court reflection, a white object on the back shutter) are the
  main reason ball tracking fails on glare courts. On a court without glare, today's bounce finder
  gets 35%, close to the 40% goal.
- **Learned:** the new filter removes almost all the glare and every glare fake rally, but slow
  balls (soft bounces, a ball rolling to a stop) look like glare to it.
- **Learned:** all 4 real rallies it lost were on one glare court, where today's system was partly
  following glare already; two of those rallies were found by luck. Clean courts lost none.
- **Decided:** the filter stays off. Nothing in the product changes.
- **Next, if pursued:** switch it on only for courts with measured glare, and only remove spots that
  stay lit for minutes, not seconds. Test on fresh footage (all three labelled stretches are now
  used up). Tracked in Linear (see PIC-226).
- **Separate problem:** on the worst glare court the ball itself is hard to see; a filter can't fix that.
