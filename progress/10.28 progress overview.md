**Found and fixed today:**
- **Stuck-start retry never fired** (production too): the orchestrator's own record-gpu write read as a check-in. Check-in is now the job's stage moving. The limit for a machine that hasn't started went from 8 to 4 min. There is also a backstop that requeues a part whose watcher died.
- **Last part rented a cold machine.** It now waits for its session's machine that is finishing the previous part.
- **Vertical framing leaned right on 720p uploads:** the ball was in calibration pixels, the frame in video pixels. Fixed in #43. The staging check moved the window centre from 0.63 to 0.47 (1080p: 0.50).
