**Investigated whether 720p is actually "good enough" for the final delivered reel (Instagram target) before committing to cutting cloud-side from a proxy.** Verified live (web search, not assumed): Instagram compresses everything server-side, but toward a 1080p target, not from a 720p floor — a 720p source stays visibly softer after their re-encode than a 1080p source would. Decision: upload 1080p anyway, given the target use case is genuinely social-sharing quality.
