**What players see now:**
- one look across all pages: chalk/court-green by day, green-black for video, ball yellow only as an accent;
- the reels feed has a thin seek line, tap-to-pause, a badge-led caption, and a back chevron when they came from the venue page;
- 傳送 (Send) shares the page link; 分享 (Share) attaches the clip;
- the sound button is hidden behind the PostHog flag `share-sound-button` (off, evaluated server-side, no player tracking);
- the venue page keeps its tab in the address; the admin box is hidden unless `?admin`.
