# Roadmap review: proposed changes (DRAFT, nothing applied in Linear yet)

2026-10-13. Based on all 226 Linear issues (143 open) in the `pic-vision` team, STRATEGY.md,
the PRD milestones and today's experiments (`EXPERIMENTS.md`, 2026-10-13 entries).

## What was found
- Linear is the only live roadmap. PRD s7 and CHECKLIST are stale by their own notes; STRATEGY says
  nothing in it is committed.
- 143 open issues, 128 in Backlog, no milestones and no target dates on any project. 1 open Urgent
  (PIC-116), 12 High.
- "Data Retention & Removal" (Urgent) and "Player-Started Recording & Consent" (High) contain no
  issues; the related work is under Venue Deployment and Legal.
- Venue Deployment is marked Low yet holds the Urgent and most High issues. The Rally Detection
  Accuracy summary still quotes precision ~0.29 (August). Clip Selection says ranking is "NOT BUILT"
  (it shipped, ADR-063).
- Quality foundations (frame rate, ball size, calibration, camera setup) are split across three
  projects at Medium and Low.

## Proposed structure: four tracks with gates (no dates proposed; set them yourself)
- **A. Foundations for quality**: frame rate and camera spec, ball visibility (detector / tracker
  comparison), calibration and lens correction, the label engine.
- **B. Sellable product**: security P0s (PIC-127, 114, 102), invoices (PIC-113), spend controls
  (PIC-80, 187), retention and consent (PIC-141, 60, 61, 62, 190), second-venue isolation (PIC-116),
  GPU cost (PIC-186).
- **C. Reel quality now**: cap each session to the top 3-4, thumbs-down with a reason, a stated promise.
- **D. New highlight types (Court Moments)**: gated on A; re-sequenced.
- Proposed gate A to D: ball tracked on at least X% of frames in serve windows at the chosen frame rate
  and camera (X to be set by the operator after the 60 fps and detector tests).
