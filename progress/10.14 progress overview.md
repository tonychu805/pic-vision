
## Bounce-labelling pilot built (tool + analysis), nothing labelled yet

Goal: find out, against hand labels, whether a 2D landing map is a near-term feature. The
ball is tracked on only ~40% of frames and the serve-bounce probe estimated a landing for 9 of
25 clips, so we do not know whether bounces are visible at all. Built on branch
`feature/bounce-labelling` (from `feature/blind-check`):

- `label_bounce_web.py`: browser tool, step frame by frame and click where the ball touches the
  floor. Blind (shows no detector output). Saves to `eval/labels/bounces/labels.jsonl`.
- `scripts/make_bounce_sample.py` chose 38 rallies: 18 PGC (judged real or serve-and-return) and
  20 public (10 brickwall_30fps, 10 pb_draft_cup).
- `scripts/eval_bounce_recovery.py`: seen / tracked / found and court error in feet, per set and
  per near / far half. The pass bar is fixed in its docstring before any label exists.

Next: label the 18 PGC clips before the PGC footage decision (~2026-10-25), then the public ones;
run the analysis; record in EXPERIMENTS.md either way. Start with
`.venv/bin/python label_bounce_web.py`, open it over `tailscale serve`.
