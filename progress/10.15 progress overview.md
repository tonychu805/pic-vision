## Bounce pilot scored on PGC: the finder finds 14% of hand-labelled bounces (bar 40%) — fails; labelling moved to CVAT

Labelled the 18 PGC pilot clips (42 bounces) in a local CVAT install instead of the home-made
labeller; scored against the bar fixed on 2026-10-14. Detail and tables:
`EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-10-15.

- **Result:** the ball is tracked at 74% of the bounces (bar 50%, pass) but the bounce finder
  recovers only 14% (bar 40%, fail). A zone-level landing map from the current finder is not a
  near-term feature on PGC footage.
- **Why:** of 36 misses, 16 had tracked points too sparse around the bounce, 11 had no tracked
  ball at all, 8 were not a local low point. Loosening the rule reaches at most 24% found with
  about 80% of candidates matching no label. The limiter is how densely the ball is detected, not
  the rule.
- **Caveats:** one venue, 42 bounces, completeness is the labeller's word, selected rallies make
  this an upper bound for detected rallies (an unselected 3-minute chunk scored 15% found and
  48% tracked).
- **CVAT:** `cvat/` is a git-ignored clone (own `.git`); runs under Docker Desktop
  (`systemctl --user start docker-desktop`, then `docker --context desktop-linux compose up -d`
  in `cvat/`). Project `bounce-pilot-pgc`, tasks 20-37 = clips pgc-303..pgc-330. Use Shape, not
  Track; export with `scripts/cvat_to_bounce_labels.py`, not CVAT's own export (it expands a
  Track to every later frame).
- **Does not change:** the PGC footage decision (~2026-10-25) turns on consent, not on this.

Next: only worth more effort if ball detection density improves; otherwise leave the landing map
parked. The 20 public clips were not labelled (they are the easy-case ceiling, not PGC).
