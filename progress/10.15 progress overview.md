## Bounce pilot scored on PGC: the finder finds 14% of hand-labelled bounces (bar 40%) — fails; labelling moved to CVAT

Labelled the 18 PGC pilot clips (42 bounces) in a local CVAT install instead of the home-made
labeller; scored against the bar fixed on 2026-10-14. Detail and tables:
`EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-10-15.

- **Result:** the ball is tracked at 74% of the bounces (bar 50%, pass) but the bounce finder
  recovers only 14% (bar 40%, fail). A zone-level landing map from the current finder is not a
  near-term feature on PGC footage.
- **Why:** of 36 misses, 16 had tracked points too sparse around the bounce, 11 had no tracked
  ball at all, 8 were not a local low point. Loosening the rule reaches at most 24% found with
  about 80% of candidates matching no label. The limiter is how densely the ball is detected, not
  the rule.
- **Caveats:** one venue, 42 bounces, completeness is the labeller's word, selected rallies make
  this an upper bound for detected rallies (an unselected 3-minute chunk scored 15% found and
  48% tracked).
- **CVAT:** `cvat/` is a git-ignored clone (own `.git`); runs under Docker Desktop
  (`systemctl --user start docker-desktop`, then `docker --context desktop-linux compose up -d`
  in `cvat/`). Project `bounce-pilot-pgc`, tasks 20-37 = clips pgc-303..pgc-330. Use Shape, not
  Track; export with `scripts/cvat_to_bounce_labels.py`, not CVAT's own export (it expands a
  Track to every later frame).
- **Does not change:** the PGC footage decision (~2026-10-25) turns on consent, not on this.

### Follow-up the same day: zoomed tiles clear the bar

Re-ran the same 42 bounces with the detector looking at two zoomed halves of the frame instead of
the whole frame (rule and bar committed first, `ff40b10`): **19 of 42 found (45%) against 8 (19%)
for the whole frame**, bar 40%. The gain is almost all on the far half of the court, where the
ball is smallest (7 -> 16 of 27). So the limiter was zoom, not the bounce rule and not frame count.

- **Not a drop-in:** it costs two passes per frame, and on public footage it hurt rally precision
  (2026-10-13). A candidate for a bounce-only pass over rallies already detected.
- **Held on an unselected stretch:** the 3-minute chunk (33 bounces, not chosen as rallies):
  14 found with tiles against 5 for the whole frame (42% vs 15%), exactly at the 40% bar, so
  not a comfortable pass. Seen rises 55% -> 88% there.
- **Still a lead, not a decision:** one venue, one labeller, borrowed calibration on the chunk.
  Next: a second camera/venue and what the unmatched candidates are, before any ADR.
- **Corrects the "not near-term" line above:** that held for the whole-frame detector.

### Later the same day: what the extra candidates were, and a hit-vs-bounce test

- **Candidate review:** of the tile arm's 37 candidates, the operator tagged 23 bounces (17
  matched labels + 6 the labels had missed), 13 paddle hits and 1 not-a-ball. So the labels were
  about 85% complete, and the finder's precision problem is paddle hits (35%), not noise.
- **Audio does not help:** the recording carries no audible bounce above the hall noise (33
  labelled bounces show no sound at any offset).
- **Motion does:** speed after the event divided by speed before separates bounces (median 0.72)
  from hits (median 1.13): AUC 0.88, leave-one-out 83%. Passes the bar registered first; one chunk,
  one camera, no held-out data, so it is a lead.
- **Next, in order:** (1) a second labelled chunk (different time or court) to confirm the
  speed-ratio threshold, (2) hits near the net/volleys, (3) only then an ADR.
