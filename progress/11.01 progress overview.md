# 11.01 progress overview

## PDSC Court 3 field test: why no rallies are found

During the Court 3 test the production reel jobs kept finishing with "no rallies found", even though people were playing. We replayed the pipeline locally on the recordings to find out why. Full numbers and method are in `EXPERIMENTS.md` (2026-11-01).

**What we know.**
- The pipeline itself is fine. On the brickwall footage it finds the ball in about a third of frames and finds rallies. On Court 3 it finds the ball in roughly 6-14% of frames and no rallies.
- It is not the frame rate (both recordings are a true 30 fps) and not the 1080p switch.
- The pale wall behind the far baseline is a major cause. In a real rally the operator marked frame by frame, the far-side ball was found 1 time in 12 while it was against the wall, and 10 times in 22 against the court. When we washed the dark wall of a good brickwall clip to cream, detection fell from 37% to 13% of frames.
- Cropping to the court gives about 3.6x more net crossings but still almost no rallies (4 candidates across 18 parts, none confirmed beyond one real rally in part 9). Zooming into the far side did not help against the wall, and contrast tweaks recovered at most 3 of the 12 wall-side balls.

**What did not work.** Replacing the static wall with a dark fill brought the wall-side ball back (7 of 12) but also produced scattered false dots; filtering those out kept 5 of 12 but lost the one real rally in part 9. A colour-based tracker was junk (it followed a fence and people). So no software fix is ready, and nothing in the pod changed.

**Next.**
1. The remaining software route is fine-tuning the detector on this venue's footage. That needs many more hand-marked ball positions than the 91 saved in `eval/labels/pdsc_court3_20261101_ball_marks.jsonl`.
2. Whichever fix is tried, score it on those marked frames first, then through staging before any change reaches the pod (rules in `CLAUDE.md`).

**Open / not verified.** The sample is one rally and 12 wall-side balls, and the neighbour filter was tuned on the same marks. We did not test the 2880x1620 stream with a pod that crops before shrinking. Court 3's camera is higher and wider than brickwall's, so the wall is probably not the only cause.

## Later the same day: a fine-tuned ball detector, ready for staging (not yet run there)

We retrained the ball detector so it can find the far-side ball against Court 3's pale wall: the existing model was shown brickwall footage with the wall randomly washed pale, using its own confident detections as the answers. Two epochs of training did best. On the pod's own conditions (whole frame, no crop), Court 3 goes from no rallies per part to about 10 candidates a part. A held-out brickwall clip does not get worse at the normal confidence setting (15 rallies against 12), so the pod now runs once at a lower setting and keeps the strict one for any video that already detects the ball well. Details and every number are in `EXPERIMENTS.md` (2026-11-01, later).

**How good is it?** Not proven. Of the first nine of the pod's candidate rallies on part 9 that the operator judged, 4 were real, 1 part real and 4 not rallies. The cropped candidates earlier were better (9 of 11 real play). Recall is unmeasured, the wall result rests on 12 marked balls, and only two venues were checked.
