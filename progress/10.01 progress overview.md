# 10.01 progress overview

## AI training consent: two proposals to weaken it, both declined, grounded in actual Taiwan PDPA text

Operator considered defaulting the `/account` AI-training checkbox to pre-checked, then considered removing it entirely and folding training consent into the general privacy-policy acceptance at signup. Both would make the checkbox (or lack of one) effectively meaningless as consent.

Rather than argue from GDPR analogy — which doesn't actually govern this business — went and found the real Taiwan 個人資料保護法 provisions: 第 20 條第 1 項第 6 款 requires the data subject's consent for any use **beyond the original collection purpose** (training an AI model is clearly outside "produce this player's own highlight reel"), and 第 7 條第 4 項 specifically requires that consent be a **"單獨"（separate/standalone）** expression of will — not bundled with anything else. A Taiwan tech-law firm's own published Q&A was found directly on point, confirming pre-ticked boxes and bundled "agree to everything at once" consent don't satisfy this.

**A more fundamental, unresolved question surfaced in the same research**: that same source states that simply clicking "我同意" on a screen does not, by itself, satisfy PDPA's "**書面**" (written) form requirement that Article 20(1)(6) explicitly demands for out-of-original-purpose use — which is exactly what AI-training consent is. Taiwan's Electronic Signatures Act generally lets electronic consent stand in for "written," but whether our specific mechanism (a checkbox, backed by a stored consent_version/IP/user-agent/timestamp row) clears that bar is a real open question, not something either a web search or this session's own reasoning can certify. **Flagged as needing an actual Taiwan data-privacy lawyer's review before relying further on the AI-training consent mechanism as currently built** — this is a question about the checkbox's legal validity as a form, not about the UX/bundling questions above, which are resolved.

Decision: AI training consent stays optional, unchecked by default, and separate from both signup and the mandatory filming/retention consent. Nothing built or changed today on this point — this was a discussion that correctly stopped before any code or policy text changed, pending the lawyer question above.
