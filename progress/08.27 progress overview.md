## Built the 720p upload proxy — found and fixed a real correctness risk before shipping it

Operator asked to shrink what the cloud route uploads to R2/RunPod down to 720p, to speed up the setup/transfer legs of a job. Before writing the ffmpeg flag, checked whether it was actually safe: `calib.json`'s `homography`/`image_points` are absolute pixel coordinates with **no resolution recorded anywhere** — `court_mask()` (`scripts/pod_infer.py`) builds its mask by projecting that homography directly against whatever frame it's given, assuming same resolution as calibration. Every video processed so far has satisfied that assumption by coincidence; a 720p proxy against a native-resolution calibration would have silently misplaced the court mask — the same class of bug as `ADR-064`'s coordinate mismatch, but corrupting what the detector sees rather than just a reported coordinate.

**Fix, recorded as `DECISIONS.md` ADR-069:** every `calib.json`-writing site (`calibrate.py`, `calibrate_web.py`, `webapp/app.py`'s two save routes) now records `calibration_resolution: [w, h]`. `court_mask()` builds the mask at the calibration's own resolution, then resizes the finished boolean mask (nearest-neighbor) to whatever frame is actually being processed — deliberately simpler than hand-scaling the intermediate row/column math, given that per-axis arithmetic is exactly what produced `ADR-064`'s bug. Output coordinates (`x_ratio`/`y_ratio`) are likewise computed against `calibration_resolution` when present, so `predictions.csv` always lands in the calibration's pixel space regardless of what resolution actually got processed — `build_reel()`, `track_ball`, `crossing_times` need zero changes, the proxy is invisible to them. Old `calib.json` files without the new field fall back to today's exact behavior; `run_cloud_job.py` refuses to use the 720p proxy for a venue calibrated before this field existed rather than risk a silent scale mismatch, uploading full-res instead with a clear warning.

**Verified, not assumed:** backward compatibility confirmed byte-identical (old-format calib.json, before vs. after the code change, same clip). New behavior confirmed on a genuine 720p downscale of a real clip against a calibration with `calibration_resolution` added: `x_ratio`/`y_ratio` and the mask coverage percentage matched the native-resolution run exactly, and of 70 frames both runs detected a ball, mean position distance was 1.8px in native-resolution space (max 39.8px, one outlier) — small, expected differences from lower-res encoding, not a systematic scale mismatch.

**Left honestly open:** whether detection *accuracy* (recall/precision against real labels) holds up at 720p versus native resolution is unmeasured — this work verifies the coordinate mechanism is correct, not that quality is unaffected. That's the next thing to check before trusting 720p in place of full-res on a real scored session.
