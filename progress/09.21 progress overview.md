# 09.21 progress overview

## The Office machine going quiet, and why "Connected" wasn't telling the truth

The Office venue machine stopped reporting to the console for over half an hour. Its own screen said "Connected" the whole time, its Log tab said nothing, and a restart fixed it. Later the same day a new camera's "Calibrate" produced no snapshot: six clicks queued, none picked up.

**What I found.** The agent does its work one thing at a time, and several of those things could wait forever — a stalled upload, a request the console never answered, a camera that accepts a connection and says nothing. When one did, everything behind it waited too, including the check-in to the console. And because waiting forever isn't an error, nothing got logged and the screen kept saying "Connected".

**What I don't know.** Which call actually hung. I never saw the Office machine's log. This fixes the whole class (nothing may wait forever), not a proven single cause — if it happens again after the next release, that's new information.

**What changed (built and tested, not released).** Every wait now has a limit. The status line says "Not checking in" when the last success is over three minutes old, instead of "Connected". On the console, a second Calibrate click reuses the pending request instead of queuing another, and snapshot requests nobody answered in ten minutes are closed as expired. See ADR-119.
