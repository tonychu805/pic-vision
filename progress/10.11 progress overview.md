# 10.11 progress overview

## Court Moments: scope narrowed after a ball-trace experiment (PIC-193)

**What was tried.** Drawing the ball's path on the video and on a top-down court from the existing ball detections (brickwall pilot set, 9 rallies, 12:43–12:51 in detail). Details and numbers: `EXPERIMENTS.md` 2026-10-11.

**What we learned, plainly.**
- From a camera behind the baseline, the far half of the court is squashed, so a ball's distance can't be told from its height. Far-end positions are the least reliable.
- When a player covers the ball near the net, the fit invents a path through the gap and can produce a false "net hit".
- Long clean flights come out well; the interesting moments (net contacts, far end, near the kitchen) are where it is weakest.

**Decisions (operator, 2026-10-11).**
- Ball traces are **not** a deliverable. The scripts are kept as internal diagnostics.
- Highlight types in scope, in order: **fast exchange** (ball only), then **kitchen battle** and **chase / recovery** (need players), then **funny moments** (falls, ball hits player) behind a consent flow.
- **Out:** score-based moments (no score source); movement analytics is a separate project (PIC-147/148).

**Linear.** PIC-195/204/205/206 parked; PIC-196 is now "do first" (fast exchange, ball-only), with PIC-219 moved under it; PIC-197 gains the occlusion finding; PIC-200 reframed as consent-gated funny moments.
