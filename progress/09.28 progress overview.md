# 09.28 progress overview

## Rally clips now get a 0–100 score (ADR-133)

Each rally clip gets a score out of 100 from how long the rally was, how fast the exchanges got, and how many hard shots it had. A session's best 10 clips on the share page are now picked by that score across the whole session, not "each 10-minute part's best, then each part's second best". Tested against the hand grades (slightly better, within noise) and re-tuned on 180 rallies of PGC footage.

**Live:** the database side. **Waiting for the operator's push:** the pipeline (`pic-vision` main), console and share page. The console must go out only after the database change, which is already done.

**Limit:** scores only compare fairly within one camera. Court 2's camera angle roughly doubles the hard-shot count against court 1's. Don't build a "best rally across all courts" feature on this yet.
