# 09.24 progress overview

## Rehearsal of sending a game in 10-minute parts: it works, and it found one real bug (fixed)

A full test ran against the real cloud, using a stand-in camera that played real Court 4 footage in a loop, a separate test venue and 10-minute parts:

- **Parts went out while recording.** Each 10-minute piece uploaded in under 2 minutes on this connection.
- **After Stop, the last part's reels were ready in about 10 minutes.**
- **One link for the whole game:** the reels from the parts landed together on the same share page.

**The bug:** a 10-minute stretch with almost no play made the job **fail** instead of saying "no rallies". It was always possible, but rare when a whole session was sent at once. With 10-minute parts it'll happen at every break. Now such a stretch finishes normally with "no rallies found", and the rest of the game's reels are unaffected. Confirmed on the rehearsal's own footage.

**Worth knowing for the weekend:**
- **Venue upload speed** is still the big unknown. This office's fast mode keeps up easily; its slow mode (seen before) wouldn't. Check it with the Diagnostics tab at the venue.
- **One court is fine.** Several courts all sending 10-minute parts at once would start to queue: each part takes ~15–17 minutes of cloud time, and 4 run at a time.
- **The venue computer still needs the new desktop release** for the one-link-per-game part.
